Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Skate Dice does not collect, store or share any personal information.

  • No data collection. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.
  • On-device settings only. The app remembers whether you turned sound on. That setting stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.
  • No third parties. No data is sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Children. Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
