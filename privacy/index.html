Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Skate Dice does not collect or share any personal information. Nothing leaves your device.

  • No data collection. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.
  • On-device settings only. The app remembers whether you turned sound on, which trick faces you switched off in the trick filter, and the skater names you enter for a game of S.K.A.T.E. These stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • History isn't saved. The list of tricks you've rolled is kept only while the app is open and is cleared when you close it.
  • No third parties. No data is sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • Children. Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
