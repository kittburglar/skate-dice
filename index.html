Skate Dice

Throw the dice, do the trick. Four 3D dice — stance, direction, spin and trick — tumble across the table and land on a flatground trick to try, with the name skaters actually call it.

How to play

  • Tap Throw or shake your phone.
  • An X or a skateboard on a die leaves that die out.
  • Three of them? Do the trick that's left and add your own variation.
  • All four X: choose any trick. All four skateboards: take a letter away from yourself or give one to someone else.
